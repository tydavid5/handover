# Privacy Policy for Handover

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Handover is a Forge app built by Taiwo John for the Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It builds a leave-handover pack for a teammate from their Jira work, Confluence pages and Teamwork Graph context, and a Rovo agent answers questions about that pack.

## What the app reads

When someone asks for a handover pack, the app reads, inside the Atlassian site where it is installed:

- the named person's Jira work items (key, summary, status, priority, due date) and the comments on them,
- the Confluence pages that person contributed to,
- the person's teams and goals from the Teamwork Graph.

It only reads the data needed to build the pack. It does not read data for anyone else, and it does not read message content outside work items and their comments.

## What the app stores

The generated pack is saved in Forge storage, which is hosted by Atlassian, so the project page can show it again. Nothing is sent to any server outside Atlassian, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties. The app has no analytics and no tracking.
